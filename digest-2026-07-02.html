The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries

The Takeaway: The combination of pre-training plus reinforcement learning is what makes modern AI systems capable of genuine mathematical discovery — not just bigger models.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts argues that AI has crossed a threshold where it can solve problems that humans haven't solved yet, using the Erdos problems as a key example. The secret sauce isn't scale alone — it's reinforcement learning, which he compares to learning by doing versus watching demonstrations. Roberts frames intelligence as becoming increasingly customizable rather than a monolithic "bigger is better" race. He also contrasts OpenAI's informal, intuition-driven approach to math proofs with DeepMind's formal Lean-based system, suggesting both paths have merit but serve different purposes.
