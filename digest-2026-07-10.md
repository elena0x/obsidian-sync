### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**"OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries"**

**The Takeaway:** Reinforcement learning — learning by doing, not just watching — is the paradigm that's enabling AI to make genuine mathematical discoveries.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts sits down to explain how AI cracked the Erdős problems, a class of mathematical challenges that had resisted human solution. The breakthrough wasn't just scale; it was a shift in how AI learns. Roberts draws a sharp distinction between pre-training (watching demonstrations) and reinforcement learning (trial and error with feedback) — and argues the combination is what makes modern systems so powerful.

He also weighs in on the OpenAI vs. DeepMind approaches to math: OpenAI's informal, proof-style reasoning versus DeepMind's formal Lean-based verification. Roberts makes the case that intelligence is becoming customizable — the winners won't necessarily have the biggest models, but the ones that can adapt intelligence to specific domains.

The episode is a rare window into how frontier labs think about the boundary between pattern-matching and genuine discovery.
