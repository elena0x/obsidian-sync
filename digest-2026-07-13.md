### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries**

**The Takeaway:** Reinforcement learning is the paradigm shift that lets AI systems make genuine mathematical discoveries, not just regurgitate patterns.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts sits down with Matt Turck to discuss a breakthrough that sounds like science fiction: AI solving Erdős problems in mathematics. Roberts frames reinforcement learning as the key — it's the difference between learning by doing versus passively watching demonstrations. This isn't about scaling up model size; it's about a fundamentally different way of training.

Roberts argues that intelligence is becoming customizable rather than just about bigger models. The real power comes from combining pre-training with RL — pre-training gives you broad knowledge, RL gives you the ability to reason and discover. He contrasts OpenAI's informal approach to math proofs with DeepMind's formal Lean-based methodology, suggesting both paths have merit but serve different purposes.

The conversation has clear implications beyond math: if AI can make novel discoveries in one of humanity's most rigorous fields, what happens when this capability generalizes to drug discovery, materials science, or engineering?
