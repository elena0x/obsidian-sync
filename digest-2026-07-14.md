### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries**

**The Takeaway:** Reinforcement learning is the key paradigm shift that enables AI to make genuine mathematical discoveries, not just regurgitate patterns.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts explains how modern AI systems are solving mathematical problems that stumped humans for decades, particularly the Erdős problems. The breakthrough isn't just scale — it's the combination of pre-training with reinforcement learning (RL). Roberts describes RL as "learning by doing" versus the older paradigm of "learning by watching demonstrations."

He argues intelligence is becoming increasingly customizable rather than simply about building bigger models. The pre-training + RL combination is what makes today's systems capable of genuine reasoning. Roberts also contrasts approaches: OpenAI favors informal math proofs while DeepMind pursues formal verification through Lean.

> "Intelligence is becoming customizable rather than just about bigger models."

This episode is essential listening for anyone trying to understand where AI reasoning is actually headed — beyond the hype about model size.
