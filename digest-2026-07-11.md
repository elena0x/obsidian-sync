## PODCASTS

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck: OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries**

*The Takeaway:* Reinforcement learning is the key paradigm that enables AI to make genuine discoveries, not just bigger models.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts argues that the real breakthrough in AI isn't just scale — it's the shift from learning by watching to learning by doing. Roberts, who works on AI's ability to solve mathematical problems (including the famous Erdos problems), explains that pre-training plus reinforcement learning is what makes modern systems capable of genuine discovery. He contrasts OpenAI's more informal approach to mathematical proofs with DeepMind's formal Lean-based verification system. The key insight: intelligence isn't just about having more knowledge — it's about the ability to explore, fail, and improve through feedback, which is exactly what RL enables.
