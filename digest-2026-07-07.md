## PODCASTS

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries**

The Takeaway: Reinforcement learning — not just bigger models — is what enables AI to make genuine mathematical discoveries.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts makes a compelling case that we've entered a new era where AI can solve problems that stump even expert mathematicians. The key isn't scale alone; it's the combination of pre-training plus reinforcement learning (RL). Roberts compares RL to learning by doing — you improve through trial and error and feedback — versus simply watching demonstrations. This is the paradigm that allowed AI systems to crack Erdos problems, long-standing mathematical challenges.

Roberts also draws an interesting contrast between approaches: OpenAI favors an informal, flexible style of mathematical reasoning, while DeepMind pursues formal proofs using the Lean programming language. Both paths are valid, but they reflect different philosophies about what "understanding" means in mathematics.

Perhaps his most important insight: intelligence is becoming customizable. The winners won't necessarily be the labs with the biggest models, but those who can shape intelligence into something purpose-built for specific domains.
