### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries

**The Takeaway:** Reinforcement learning — not just bigger models — is the paradigm that's enabling AI to make genuine mathematical discoveries.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts sits down to explain why AI is suddenly solving mathematical problems that stumped humans for decades, particularly the Erdős problems. The secret isn't scale alone — it's reinforcement learning, which he compares to learning by doing versus watching demonstrations. Pre-training gives models the raw pattern-matching ability; RL teaches them to think through problems step by step, correcting course when wrong.

Roberts makes a sharp distinction between approaches: OpenAI favors an informal, human-readable style of mathematical reasoning, while DeepMind pursues formal proofs using Lean, a proof assistant that guarantees correctness. Both have merit — informal proofs are more accessible and faster to produce, while formal proofs eliminate doubt entirely.

The broader implication: intelligence is becoming customizable. The winning companies won't be those with the biggest models, but those who can turn intelligence into something purpose-built for their domain. The combination of pre-training plus RL is what makes modern AI systems feel qualitatively different from what came before.
