### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries

**The Takeaway:** Reinforcement learning is the key paradigm that lets AI systems go from watching demonstrations to actually learning by doing — and that's why they're now capable of making genuine mathematical discoveries.

OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts breaks down how AI recently solved Erdős problems in mathematics, a feat that required more than just pattern-matching. The breakthrough came from combining pre-training with reinforcement learning (RL), where the system learns by trying things, failing, and adjusting — much like a human mathematician wrestling with a proof.

Roberts makes a sharp distinction between two approaches to formalizing math with AI: OpenAI's informal, intuition-driven style versus DeepMind's rigorous Lean-based formal proofs. The informal approach moves faster and captures the messy, creative side of mathematical thinking. The formal approach is slower but guarantees correctness.

He also echoes a broader theme we're seeing across the industry: intelligence is becoming customizable, not just bigger. The winning systems won't be the ones with the most parameters — they'll be the ones that can adapt their reasoning to specific problems through RL and targeted training.
